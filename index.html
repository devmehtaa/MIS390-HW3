ABOUT OUR PRACTICE

Dental Care You Can Trust

Mountain View Dental Clinic is a family-oriented dental practice dedicated to providing excellent dental care to patients of all ages.

Founded ten years ago by Dr. Joshi Michailoff, our clinic combines professional experience with a welcoming environment. We believe every patient deserves personalized attention and comfortable dental care.

Dr. Michailoff graduated from the University of Texas Health Science Center with a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. He is also a Fellow of the Academy of General Dentistry, a prestigious professional distinction.

WE ARE HERE TO HELP

Dental Emergency?

Dental emergencies can happen unexpectedly. Dr. Michailoff may be paged after regular office hours for emergency care. Same-day care is provided for most emergencies.

If you experience a dental emergency, contact the clinic during office hours for assistance. For life-threatening emergencies, call 911 or visit the nearest emergency department.
